L'addition

The expensive part of a games night isn't the game.

It's the deciding. Two ways through the same evening — same library, same ninety minutes:

Browsing the store
~40 min
  • Four storefronts, three wishlists
  • Sales you can't tell are actually sales
  • Reviews for a game you already own
  • Something long, started at 22:40
Asking GameGusto
~1 min
  • It already knows your library
  • It checks your regional store, in your currency
  • It fits the time you actually have
  • You play for eighty-nine minutes

Over a month of evenings, that's the difference between playing and shopping. The games were always there — the hours went to finding them.
